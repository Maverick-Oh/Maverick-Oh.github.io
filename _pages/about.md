I am a Assistant Professor at Montgomery College teaching Physics and Astronomy.

I worked with <a href='https://naturalsciences.ucmerced.edu/people/anna-nierenberg'>Prof. Anna Nierenberg</a> on modeling and analysis of gravitationally lensed quasar systems to understand and constrain dark matter properties with non-elliptical galaxy shape perturbation.

I am also enrolled in the Graduate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning program at The University of Texas at Austin as a recipient of the CosmicAI Scholarship.

I also have worked on other projects related to the graphical notation of vector calculus, physics-informed machine learning, and Korean science-inspired poems.

Please feel free to reach out.

Thanks!
