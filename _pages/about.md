I am an Assistant Professor of Physics and Astronomy at Montgomery College.

I earned my PhD in 2026 at <a href="https://www.ucmerced.edu/">University of California, Merced</a>. My PhD research focuses on gravitationally lensed quasar systems and their applications to understanding the properties of dark matter. I worked with <a href="https://naturalsciences.ucmerced.edu/people/anna-nierenberg">Prof. Anna Nierenberg</a> on modeling and measuring non-elliptical perturbations in galaxy distributions and studying their effects on gravitational-lensing observations.

I am currently enrolled in the <a href="https://cdso.utexas.edu/caiml">Graduate Certificate in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning</a> program at <a href="https://www.utexas.edu/">The University of Texas at Austin</a> as a recipient of <a href="https://cosmicai.org/graduatecertificate">the CosmicAI Scholarship</a>.

My other projects have explored graphical notation for vector calculus, physics-informed machine learning, and Korean science-inspired poetry.

Please feel free to reach out!
